Privacy Policy

App: Beat Studio · Last updated: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beat Studio (“the app”, “we”) handles your information. We built Beat Studio to respect your privacy: the app works fully offline and does not require an account.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any account.

2. Data stored on your device

Beats you record and your in-app settings (such as selected kit, volume, haptics preference, and purchase status) are saved only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or any third party, and is removed if you delete the app.

3. Purchases

Beat Studio offers an optional one-time “Pro” upgrade. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Please review the store’s privacy policy for how they handle payment information.

4. Analytics & tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Network access

The app functions offline. The only time it accesses the internet is if you tap an external link (such as this policy), which opens in your browser.

6. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us at tech@clk.company.
